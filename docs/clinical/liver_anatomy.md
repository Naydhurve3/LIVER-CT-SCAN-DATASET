# Liver Anatomy for CT Analysis

## Overview

The liver is the largest solid organ in the human body, located in the right upper quadrant of the abdomen. It plays a crucial role in metabolism, detoxification, and protein synthesis.

## Segmental Anatomy (Couinaud Classification)

The liver is divided into 8 functional segments based on vascular supply:

| Segment | Location | Key Landmarks |
|---------|----------|---------------|
| I (Caudate) | Posterior, between IVC and portal vein | Independent blood supply |
| II | Left lateral superior | Above portal vein bifurcation |
| III | Left lateral inferior | Below portal vein bifurcation |
| IVa | Left medial superior | Above portal vein bifurcation |
| IVb | Left medial inferior | Below portal vein bifurcation |
| V | Right anterior inferior | Below portal vein bifurcation |
| VI | Right posterior inferior | Below portal vein bifurcation |
| VII | Right posterior superior | Above portal vein bifurcation |
| VIII | Right anterior superior | Above portal vein bifurcation |

## CT Appearance

- **Normal liver**: Homogeneous attenuation (50-70 HU)
- **Contrast phases**:
  - Non-contrast: 50-70 HU
  - Arterial phase: 20-30 sec post-injection
  - Portal venous phase: 60-70 sec (peak liver enhancement)
  - Delayed phase: 3-5 min

## Common Pathologies Detectable on CT

1. **Hepatocellular Carcinoma (HCC)** — Most common primary liver malignancy
2. **Metastases** — Most common liver malignancy (from colon, breast, lung, etc.)
3. **Hemangioma** — Benign vascular lesion
4. **Cysts** — Simple fluid-filled lesions
5. **Cirrhosis** — Diffuse nodular regeneration
6. **Steatosis** — Fatty infiltration
